# ISO 6892-1:2019 Atomic RTM - Annex A

## Control statement

This inventory paraphrases informative Annex A of the controlled English third edition. It does not reproduce the standard. Printed-page and PDF-page locators identify the controlled local source. Annex A recommendations remain informative unless a controlled product standard, agreement or method profile makes a selected procedure governing.

`EXTRACTED / REVIEW-PENDING` means that the source item is independently addressable and cross-linked, but its interpretation and parameterization have not received independent scientific sign-off. No row is implementation, validation execution or conformity evidence.
