# ISO 6892-1:2019 Atomic RTM - Annex D

## Control statement

This inventory paraphrases normative Annex D, Formula D.1, Tables D.1-D.3 and Figure 13 of the controlled English third edition. It does not reproduce the standard. Printed-page and PDF-page locators identify the controlled local source. Rectangular-specimen Figure 11 is already controlled by the Annex-B package; Annex-D rectangular geometry shall reference `ISO19-AB-F11-001` through `ISO19-AB-F11-007` instead of duplicating those source items.

`EXTRACTED / REVIEW-PENDING` means that the source item is independently addressable and cross-linked, but its interpretation and parameterization have not received independent scientific sign-off. No row is implementation, validation execution or conformity evidence.
