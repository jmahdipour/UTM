# ISO 6892-1:2019 Atomic RTM - Annex E

## Control statement

This inventory paraphrases normative Annex E, Formulas E.1-E.4 and Figures 14-15 of the controlled English third edition. It does not reproduce the standard. Printed-page and PDF-page locators identify the controlled local source. Machined strip and circular specimens route to the already controlled Annex-B or Annex-D packages at the stated tube-wall-thickness boundary; their detailed geometry rows are not duplicated here.

`EXTRACTED / REVIEW-PENDING` means that the source item is independently addressable and cross-linked, but its interpretation and parameterization have not received independent scientific sign-off. No row is implementation, validation execution or conformity evidence.
