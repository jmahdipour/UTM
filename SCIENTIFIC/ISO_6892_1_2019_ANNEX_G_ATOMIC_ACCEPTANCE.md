## Variant count and closure rule

- Atomic acceptance variants: **24**.
- Every linked source item, formula, table row and boundary must execute; one passing nominal regression or uncertainty example cannot close a variant.
- Normative requirements, permitted alternatives, recommendations, notes, example data, proficiency evidence and historical reproducibility remain separate authority classes.
- An applicable unexecuted variant makes the parent case `BLOCKED` or `FAIL`, never `PASS`.
