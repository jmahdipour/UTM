# ISO 6892-1:2019 Atomic RTM - Annex G

## Control statement

This inventory paraphrases normative Annex G, Formulas G.1-G.8 and Tables G.1-G.3 of the controlled English third edition. It does not reproduce the standard. Printed-page and PDF-page locators identify the controlled local source. Mandatory requirements, permitted alternatives, recommendations, notes and example-only data remain distinct. The intrinsic modulus of elasticity `E` is never silently equated with the elastic-line slope `mE` used for proof-strength construction.

`EXTRACTED / REVIEW-PENDING` means that the source item is independently addressable and cross-linked, but its interpretation and parameterization have not received independent scientific sign-off. No row is implementation, validation execution or conformity evidence.
